              Strange-horned chameleon The strange-horned chameleon (Kinyongia xenorhina), also known as the Rwenzori plate-nosed chameleon, is native to the rainforests of the Ruwenzori Mountains in western Uganda and eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo. This species is named for the large protuberance on the top of its snout, particularly prominent on males. The strange-horned chameleon is olive to brown in color, with males being more olive and females more brown. They can reach a total length of 28 cm (11 in) and have among the sharpest teeth and longest claws of any chameleon species. The species is near threatened and is listed in CITES Appendix II, indicating that any international trade in this species should be carefully monitored.
